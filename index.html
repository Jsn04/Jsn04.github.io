01About

The through-line

Since my second year of engineering I have been circling one question: what does it take to make an AI system work in deployment, in front of real users, and not just when it is being evaluated? I did not always think this way. For a couple of years I treated a high benchmark score as proof that a model worked. Most of what I have learned since is about why that is not enough.

I have answered pieces of it as an engineer at NVIDIA, as the founder of a live AI product, and as an applied AI engineer for an enterprise client. The same concern runs through my four research papers, from driver safety to data privacy to LLM security.

I also represent Team India in inline skating. That is not separate from the work. AthleteIQ exists because I have lived the coach and athlete dynamic from the inside.
