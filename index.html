IEEE TPS 2025
When Prompt-Injection Detectors Meet New Data: A Leakage-Free Cross-Corpus Evaluation

Shows that near-perfect benchmark accuracy of prompt-injection detectors is largely an artifact of in-distribution evaluation. Across five independently sourced corpora, attack recall drops from 0.92 in-distribution to 0.61 cross-corpus. Proposes a leakage-free evaluation protocol using a contamination audit. Awaiting review result.
