Under the Hood

How It Works

01 · ACWR
Workload Ratio

Acute-to-Chronic Workload Ratio measures how hard an athlete trained this week against their 28-day baseline. A ratio above 1.3 signals overreach.

ACWR = acute(7d) ÷ (chronic(28d) ÷ 4)
02 · Z-SCORE
Baseline Deviation

Each athlete's energy, mood, and sleep ratings are benchmarked against their personal historical baseline. Deviations from their own norm score higher than absolute thresholds.

z = (x − μ_athlete) ÷ σ_athlete
03 · DECEPTION
Effort Mismatch Detection

If a coach's RPE rating and the athlete's own energy self-report diverge significantly, the system flags it. Athletes playing through pain or sandbagging are caught automatically.

|RPE_coach − (10 − energy_athlete)| ≥ 3

These three components feed into a composite score (max 100) weighted across 5 pillars: ACWR (40%), baseline z (30%), deception (15%), notes sentiment (10%), vitals (5%).
