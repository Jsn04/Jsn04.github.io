Under the Hood

How It Works

01 · ACWR
Workload Ratio

Acute-to-Chronic Workload Ratio measures how hard an athlete trained this week against their 28-day baseline. Above 1.3 signals overreach and injury risk.

ACWR = acute(7d) ÷ (chronic(28d) ÷ 4)
02 · Z-SCORE
Baseline Deviation

Energy, mood, and sleep ratings are benchmarked against each athlete's personal baseline — deviations from their own norm score higher than fixed thresholds.

z = (x − μ_athlete) ÷ σ_athlete
03 · DETECT
Effort Mismatch

If a coach's RPE rating and the athlete's self-reported energy diverge beyond the threshold, the system flags it. Athletes pushing through pain get caught automatically.

|RPE_coach − (10 − energy)| ≥ 3

Composite score (max 100) across 5 pillars — ACWR (40%), baseline z (30%), deception (15%), notes sentiment (10%), vitals (5%).

What the Platform Surfaces

78
RED ZONE · HIGH RISK

ACWR at 1.6 — well above the 1.3 threshold. Coach RPE 8/10 but athlete self-reported 9/10 energy. Deception flag triggered. Notes sentiment classified as anxious. Coach alert sent automatically.

10
GREEN ZONE · OPTIMAL

ACWR at 0.9 — well within safe range. Energy, mood, and sleep all within 0.5 standard deviations of personal baseline. No coach/athlete mismatch. Cleared for full-intensity training.
